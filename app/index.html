    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official descriptions and the published roadmaps cited in each trophy's guide. One playthrough is enough — the game continues in free-roam after the final mission — but there is no mission replay, so keep manual saves at every chapter boundary (the game gives you 15 manual slots and one autosave). Never activate cheats on a trophy run: they disable trophies and saving.

    Before you start

    • Saves: park a save at the start and end of each chapter, and rotate the spare slots while exploring. Your insurance for the one missable trophy hangs on a Chapter 1 save.
    • The one missable: Positive Mental Attitude — accept Lucia's meditation invitation at Jason's house during Chapter 1 (see the Missables view).
    • No difficulty setting and no gold-medal requirement on story missions: play the story at your own pace.
    • Trackers: PlayStation tracks counts for the grind trophies (stunts, challenges, scans, money). Where a trophy's threshold is lower than the 100% checklist's, both are noted in its guide.

    Missable trophies

    Checked against the official descriptions and the published guides cited below, exactly one of the 55 trophies can be permanently missed. Everything else stays available in free-roam, including after the final mission.

    Why only one?

    The story trophies unlock automatically as missions end, the stranger strands and activities stay on the map, and the game continues after the credits — so nothing else can be locked away. The remaining risk is self-inflicted: the game has no mission replay, so chapter-boundary saves are still worth keeping, and cheats disable trophies and saving for the session.
